Terms of Use

1. About this build

Keepit is a browser extension for bilingual translation, reading, notes, highlights, dictionary lookup, video study, speaking and listening practice, and AI assistance. This is a demo build: it is distributed as an unpacked extension for testing. Features, storage format and behaviour may change or break without notice.

2. Your account

The account you create in Keepit exists only on this device. It is not registered anywhere, cannot be recovered, and does not follow you to another browser or computer.

  • You are responsible for the device the account lives on.
  • Use a real email only if you are comfortable storing it locally. Any password works; do not reuse a password you use elsewhere.
  • To remove the account, delete it in Settings → Account, or remove the extension. There is no “password reset” — nothing to reset it from.

3. Acceptable use

Use Keepit only in ways that are lawful where you live and that respect the sites you visit. In particular, do not use it to:

  • reproduce or redistribute copyrighted material beyond what the law allows;
  • defeat paywalls, access controls, or terms of a website you are using it on;
  • harass, stalk, or process other people’s personal data without their consent;
  • build a competing dataset by scraping translated or generated output.

4. Your content

Translations, notes, highlights, documents and dictation results you create stay yours. We claim no licence over them, because we never receive them. You are responsible for keeping backups if you care about the data — clearing site data or removing the extension deletes it permanently.

5. Third-party services

Some features hand text to services that are not ours. When you translate, the text you selected is sent to a translation provider. When you use Ask AI, the content you attach is sent through the AI service you chose. Those services have their own terms and privacy policies, and your use of them is subject to those documents, not this one.

Privacy Policy

1. Summary

Keepit is local-first. We have no server that stores your profile, your notes, your documents or your usage. We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking pixels. The only data that leaves your device is the text you deliberately send to a translation or AI service.

2. What stays on your device

Stored in your browser’s local storage for this extension, and nowhere else:

  • the account you create — email address (or username) and the password you typed;
  • your settings, interface language and theme;
  • documents you add, plus their extracted text;
  • notes, highlights, saved words and study progress;
  • dictation and listening results, and the counters shown on the dashboard.

About the password: in this demo build it is kept in local storage as you typed it, not hashed and not verified by a server. That is why you should never use a password that protects anything else.

3. What leaves your device

  • Translation: the text you select, plus the source and target language, is sent to a translation provider (for example Google Translate, MyMemory or a Lingva instance) so it can return a translation.
  • Ask AI: the page context or text you attach, and your prompt, are handed to the AI service you open it in.
  • Nothing else. No profile, no notes, no document list, no history of visited pages goes anywhere.

4. What we never collect

  • no account is created on our side, so there is no server-side profile to breach;
  • no analytics, crash reporting, session recording or A/B testing;
  • no advertising identifiers, and no data sold or shared with data brokers;
  • no reading of page content beyond what a feature needs at the moment you use it.

5. Permissions and why we ask for them

  • storage, unlimitedStorage — to keep your account, notes, documents and settings locally.
  • activeTab, tabs, scripting — to translate, highlight and read the page you are on, when you ask.
  • contextMenus — for right-click actions such as translating a selection.
  • sidePanel, offscreen — for the AI side panel and for audio playback of dictation and reading.
  • declarativeNetRequest — to adjust request headers needed by the translation and reading features.
  • Host access — the extension can run on any site, because it has to work wherever you read. It only acts when you invoke a feature.

6. Retention and deletion

Data stays until you delete it. Deleting the account in Settings → Account clears the profile; removing the extension, or clearing this site’s data in your browser settings, deletes everything else. We hold no copy, so there is nothing for us to delete on request.

7. Your choices

  • Turn off translation, or use Keepit only on sites you choose.
  • Keep sensitive documents out of the library, since they are stored unencrypted in the browser profile.
  • Use the demo without an email you care about.

8. Children

Keepit is not directed at children under 13, and because no data is collected centrally, there is no child-specific data to handle.
